## Request

Send a supported MP4, MOV, WebM, Matroska, MPEG or AVI file and a JSON reference
object that is not empty. Your
API key needs the `watermarks:embed` permission.

```sh
curl --fail-with-body https://api.etchv.com/watermarks/videos/async \
  -H "X-API-Key: $ETCHV_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: campaign-preview-42" \
  -F 'file=@preview.mp4' \
  --form-string 'data={"delivery":"campaign-preview-42"}'
```

This returns a `202` receipt. [Collect the result](/docs/api/async#retrieve-a-pending-result-either-mode)
with a key from the same Organization. The finished file keeps its container,
frame timing and supported audio. The audio is not watermarked.

## Choose sync or async

| Operation | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| Embed | `POST /watermarks/videos` |
| Detect | `POST /watermarks/videos/detect` with `file` and `watermarks:detect` |
| Background embed / detect | Append `/async` to either endpoint |

A sync request waits briefly and returns `202` if the work is still running. Both
operations run as durable jobs, which keep going even if your connection drops.
Optional query parameters: `accelerator=gpu` on Business and Enterprise plans,
`webhook_id` for async requests, and `storage_destination_id` and `storage_key` for
embedding.
[All options](/docs/api/embed#request-options).

## <span id="first-supported-profile" />Supported files

All limits apply at once. For example, the 240-frame cap allows 10 seconds at 24 fps.

| Requirement | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Container / codec | One progressive track: YUV 4:2:0 8-bit H.264 in MP4 or MOV; YUV 4:2:0 8- or 10-bit HEVC (Main, Main 10) in MP4, MOV or Matroska; YUV 4:2:0 8- or 10-bit VP9 (Profile 0, Profile 2) in WebM, MP4 or Matroska; ProRes 422 (Proxy, LT, Standard, HQ) or 4444 (4444, XQ, with or without alpha) in MOV; YUV 4:2:0 MPEG-1 video in an MPEG program stream (.mpg); YUV 4:2:0 MPEG-2 Main or Simple video in a program stream (.mpg) or transport stream (.ts); YUV 4:2:0 MPEG-4 Part 2 Simple or Advanced Simple video in MP4; or YUV 4:2:0 8-bit H.264, MPEG-4 Part 2 (including XviD and DivX) or MPEG-1/2 video in AVI |
| Timing | Constant 1–60 fps, starts at zero; no rotation |
| Duration / frames | 120 seconds / 240 frames |
| Dimensions | Even dimensions, square pixels; 1 MP per frame, 40 MP total |
| Upload / output / detection | 20 MB / 100 MB / 95 MB in one request, 100 MB through a [large-file upload](/docs/api/uploads) |
| Audio | None, or one synchronized mono/stereo track: AAC-LC in MP4; AAC-LC or 16/24-bit PCM in MOV; Opus or Vorbis in WebM; AAC-LC, Opus or Vorbis in Matroska; MP2, MP3 or AC-3 in a program stream; MP2, AAC-LC or AC-3 in a transport stream; PCM, MP2, MP3 or AC-3 in AVI |

HDR, interlaced video, variable frame rates, fragmented MP4, VP9 with alpha, other
codecs, extra tracks, subtitles and attachments are not supported. Video must use a constant frame rate.

<Callout title="Check playback compatibility">
  The output keeps your container, codec and, in AVI, FourCC. H.264, HEVC and VP9
  are lossless at the source's bit depth with YUV 4:2:0 pixels; H.264 uses the High
  4:4:4 Predictive profile. ProRes keeps its profile, chroma format and alpha, and
  MPEG video its codec, with every frame coded independently. Use a player that
  supports that codec and profile; browser and hardware support varies. Lossless
  output can be larger than the source.
</Callout>

<Details title="What verification checks">

Etchv must detect the exact watermark in every frame of the final encoded video.
Each frame must also meet 35 dB PSNR, a measure of how close it looks to the
original, after allowing for color conversion and, for ProRes and MPEG, their own
encoding.

In 10-bit video the watermark is in the upper 8 bits of each sample and the source's
finer bits are kept. ProRes 4444 and XQ are delivered with 10-bit color and alpha, so
a 12-bit source keeps its upper 10 bits.

Color tags, audio packets and timing are copied and checked. In MPEG program streams,
audio stays within one frame of its original position relative to the video. Container
metadata is copied where supported, but not byte-for-byte. Editing, transcoding or
recompression can affect recovery.

</Details>

## Durable embedding and detection

- **Embed result:** native video bytes at `/watermarks/jobs/{request_id}/result`.
- **Detect result:** JSON at `/watermarks/detection-jobs/{request_id}/result`.
- **Per-frame results:** `units`, numbered from zero. The top-level watermark ID is present only when Etchv finds the same watermark in every frame.
- **Retry:** send the same upload and `Idempotency-Key`. A dropped connection does not cancel the work.

Follow `Retry-After` when polling. A video job’s deadline is 30 minutes plus 15
seconds per frame, up to 90 minutes; completed results last 24 hours. [Retry and recovery details](/docs/api/async#retry-safely).

## Credits

| Operation | CPU credits |
| --- | --- |
| Embed | 1 per started 24 frames: 24 frames cost 1, 25 cost 2, 120 cost 5, 240 cost 10 |
| Detect | 1 per file, whatever its length |

Embedding and detection are charged separately. Credits are reserved when Etchv
accepts the request, charged once on success and refunded on failure. Retries and
downloads of saved results cost no extra credits. [GPU pricing](/docs/api/gpu#credits-and-fallback).

Python: `embed_video` / `detect_video`. Node.js: `embedVideo` / `detectVideo`.
The result’s `image` field contains the video bytes. Save it with the returned
filename and content type. The SDKs check for the result until their configured
timeout.
