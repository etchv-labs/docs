## How it works

1. Send a file and a non-empty JSON object describing your record.
2. Save the returned watermark ID alongside that record in your system.
3. Distribute the watermarked file through your application.
4. Detect a recovered copy and look up its ID in your records.

The identifier is the SHA-256 digest of your canonicalized JSON. Detection
returns that digest; keep the original data in your own system.

## API base URL

```text
https://api.etchv.com
```

Send requests from your server with a scoped [API key](/docs/authentication).
Use [synchronous requests](/docs/api/async) to wait for a result, or submit a
background job and collect it through polling or [webhooks](/docs/api/webhooks).

<Callout title="Test your distribution workflow">
  Compression, resizing, and cropping can affect recovery. Test the changes your
  files will encounter. A recovered ID is an investigative signal, not proof of
  who shared a file.
</Callout>

## Keep your files connected

Original uploads and successful watermarked results appear in the
[asset library](/docs/api/assets). Etchv includes 30-day file downloads with no
storage setup; records remain until deleted. You can also
[connect your own bucket](/docs/storage) to control result retention.

Watermarking and detection use your organization’s credits. See
[plans and credits](/docs/billing) for trial access and format availability.
