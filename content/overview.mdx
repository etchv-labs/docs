## How it works

1. **Embed:** send a file and a JSON reference, such as a delivery ID.
2. **Keep:** save the returned watermark ID with your original record.
3. **Detect:** submit a recovered copy and look up its ID.

Etchv embeds a SHA-256 digest of your JSON. Detection recovers the digest, not the
original record. Editing or recompressing files can affect recovery; test your
workflow. A match identifies a copy, not who shared it.
