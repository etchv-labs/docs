## Start with a free trial

<Facts>
  <Fact label="Trial credits">200</Fact>
  <Fact label="Duration">7 days</Fact>
  <Fact label="Payment card">Not required</Fact>
</Facts>

[Sign up](/signup) and use any supported format in your personal workspace.
The trial starts at signup, never renews and creates no automatic charge.
When time or credits run out, processing pauses; your account and records remain.

<Details title="Upgrading during a trial">

Upgrade in [Billing](/dashboard/billing). Paid credits arrive in the same workspace
after payment confirmation. Unused trial credits are spent first until their
original expiry. Paid-plan format limits apply as soon as you upgrade. Additional workspaces do not
grant another trial. Normal file retention continues after the trial ends.

</Details>

Prices are USD before tax. Annual billing saves 20%. Credits arrive monthly and
expire at each allocation’s end, including on annual plans.

Plans and credits belong to an organization. Members share its allowance;
only the owner can change billing. Image access includes supported animation,
TIFF pages and Photoshop layers. Format restrictions apply to both embedding and
detection, in the dashboard and API; unsupported plan access returns `403` before reservation.

## What uses credits

| Operation | CPU credits |
| --- | --- |
| Image embed | 1 per file, including its frames, pages and layers |
| PDF embed | 1 per page; an eight-page PDF costs 8 |
| Video embed | 1 per started 24 frames, about a second; a 120-frame clip costs 5 |
| Detect, any image, PDF or video | 1 per file, even when no watermark is found |
| Asset management, saved downloads, job retries | No additional credits |

Embedding and detection are separate charges. Failed operations refund reserved
credits. At zero available credits, new processing returns `402`; there are no
automatic overage charges. Accepted jobs retain their reservation until finalized.

## GPU processing

Business and Enterprise can request `accelerator=gpu`. It costs **3× normal
credits** when GPU runs; CPU fallback costs the normal amount.
[GPU availability and options](/docs/api/gpu).

## Change or cancel a subscription

| Action | When it takes effect |
| --- | --- |
| Change plan or billing frequency | Next subscription renewal; no proration. Annual plans change at the end of the paid year |
| Cancel renewal | Access continues through the paid period |
| Remove a scheduled change | Select the current plan and frequency |
| Update payment details / view invoices | Open **Manage billing** |

Use [Dashboard → Billing](/dashboard/billing). Resume a canceled renewal before
scheduling a plan change. Overdue payments may pause processing.
[Contact us](/contact) for Enterprise terms, evaluations or additional capacity.
