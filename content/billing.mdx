## Start with a free trial

[Sign up](/signup) for 100 credits over seven days
in your personal workspace, with all supported formats and no credit card required.
Use the dashboard immediately with your signed-in session; create an API key only
for external integrations. The trial starts at signup and never renews. Creating
additional workspaces does not grant more trial credits.

When credits run out or seven days pass, new processing pauses. No automatic charge
or subscription is created. Your account and asset records remain; normal file
retention still applies. Upgrade in [Dashboard → Billing](/dashboard/billing) any time.
After payment is confirmed, paid credits are granted in the same workspace. Unused
trial credits remain available until their original expiry and are spent first.
Paid-plan format limits apply after upgrading.

to an organization: members share its credits, and only the owner can change
the plan, payment details, or renewal settings.

Prices are USD, before applicable taxes. Annual billing is paid upfront and saves

expire when that monthly allocation ends; annual payment does not make the entire
year's credits available immediately. Your dashboard shows the next credit reset
and subscription renewal separately.

Format access applies to embedding and detection, through both the dashboard and
the synchronous and asynchronous APIs. [Image](/docs/api/embed),
[PDF](/docs/api/documents) and [video](/docs/api/videos) limits still apply.
A format outside your plan returns `403` before credits are reserved.

## What uses credits

- A successful image or PDF operation uses one credit per file, including supported
  animation frames, pages and layers.
- A video operation uses one credit per started minute. A 61-second video costs two
  credits.
- Embedding and detection are separate operations. Detection costs credits even
  when no watermark is found.
- Failed operations release reserved credits. Automatic job retries do not create
  additional charges. Use the same idempotency key when recovering a request.
- Viewing assets and downloading saved results do not use credits.

When available credits run out, new processing requests return `402`. There are
no automatic overage charges. Requests already accepted keep their reservations
until they finish. See [request recovery](/docs/api/errors).

## Change or cancel a subscription

The workspace owner can select a new plan or billing frequency in Dashboard →
Billing. The change starts at the next subscription renewal. Current access and
credits continue until then, with no prorated adjustment. On an annual plan, that
means the end of the paid year. Choose the current plan and frequency to remove a
scheduled change.

Use **Manage billing** to update payment details, view invoices or cancel renewal.
Cancellation leaves access available through the paid period. If renewal is
canceled, resume it before scheduling a plan change. An overdue payment can pause
new processing requests; update payment details in Manage billing.
