## Start with a free trial

<Facts>
  <Fact label="Trial credits">200</Fact>
  <Fact label="Duration">7 days</Fact>
  <Fact label="Payment card">Not required</Fact>
</Facts>

[Sign up](/signup) and use any supported format in your personal workspace.
The trial starts when you sign up. It never renews and never charges you
automatically. When the time or credits run out, processing pauses. Your account
and records stay.

<Details title="Upgrading during a trial">

Upgrade in [Billing](/dashboard/billing). Paid credits arrive in the same workspace
once your payment is confirmed. Etchv spends unused trial credits first, until they
would have expired. Your paid plan's format limits apply as soon as you upgrade.
Extra workspaces do not get another trial.

Trial files are kept for 30 days. If you upgrade before a file expires, Etchv keeps
it for a year from the day it was created.

</Details>

Prices are in USD before tax. Annual billing saves 20%. Credits arrive each month.
Each month's credits expire when that monthly allocation ends, including on Annual plans.

Plans and credits belong to an Organization. Members share its allowance. Only the
owner can change billing. Image access includes supported animation, TIFF pages
and Photoshop layers.

Format limits apply to both embedding and detection, in the dashboard and the API.
If your plan does not include a format, the request returns `403` before any credits
are reserved.

## What uses credits

| Operation | CPU credits |
| --- | --- |
| Image embed | 1 per file, including its frames, pages and layers |
| PDF embed | 1 per page; an eight-page PDF costs 8 |
| Video embed | 1 per started 24 frames, about a second; a 120-frame clip costs 5 |
| Detect, any image, PDF or video | 1 per file, even when no watermark is found |
| Asset management, saved downloads, job retries | No additional credits |

Embedding and detection are charged separately. If an operation fails, its reserved
credits are refunded. With zero available credits, new processing returns `402`.
Etchv never charges automatically for going over. A job that was accepted keeps
its reserved credits until it finishes.

## GPU processing

Business and Enterprise can request `accelerator=gpu`. It costs **3× normal
credits** when the GPU runs. If the work falls back to the CPU, it costs the
normal amount.
[GPU availability and options](/docs/api/gpu).

## Change or cancel a subscription

| Action | When it takes effect |
| --- | --- |
| Change plan or billing frequency | Next subscription renewal; no proration. Annual plans change at the end of the paid year |
| Cancel renewal | Access continues through the paid period. Files in Etchv storage stay for 30 days after it ends |
| Remove a scheduled change | Select the current plan and frequency |
| Update payment details | Open **Manage billing** |
| View or download invoices | The workspace owner sees them under **Invoices** on the Billing page; each opens its Stripe PDF |

Use [Dashboard → Billing](/dashboard/billing). If you canceled renewal, resume it
before you schedule a plan change. Overdue payments may pause processing.
[Contact us](/contact) for Enterprise terms, evaluations or additional capacity.

## Enterprise invoices

Enterprise contracts are invoiced by Etchv on agreed payment terms. Each
invoice carries your company's legal name, address, tax ID and PO number, and
is emailed to your billing contact. Pay it from the invoice by bank transfer,
ACH direct debit or card.

Each period's credits arrive when its invoice is issued. If an invoice is not paid
by its due date, processing pauses until it is paid. The workspace owner sees every
invoice on the Billing page. [Contact us](/contact) to set up or change a contract.

## Monthly usage statements

[Dashboard → Usage](/dashboard/history) summarizes each calendar month (UTC) by
operation, media format and hardware, with the credits used. Choose a month to
see it. Select **Download CSV** to get one row per operation. Owners and admins see
the whole workspace; members see their own requests. Statements cover the
current month so far and the eleven before it.
